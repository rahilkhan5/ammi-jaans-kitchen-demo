# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static pages, fast on cheap phones, good for Google), small client scripts only where the demo needs them, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Same stack as the other demo sites.

## Users

- **Real audience: home chefs, food bloggers, cooking channels and restaurants in Pakistan** whom the software house shows this demo to, usually on a phone over WhatsApp. They judge whether their own recipes would look this good and be this easy to follow.
- **Story audience: people cooking at home in Pakistan**, often reading the recipe on a phone propped up in the kitchen, with messy hands, cooking for family or guests.

## Product Purpose

A demonstration recipe blog for a fictional Pakistani home cook, built by the software house to show what a blog from them looks like and does: many posts, categories, search, and recipe pages that are easy to cook from. Success: a food creator asks for one like it.

## Positioning

A recipe blog you can actually cook from: the recipe comes first (no long story before it), ingredients scale for the number of people, steps can be ticked off, and the screen stays readable in the kitchen, instead of a slow blog full of ads where the recipe is at the bottom.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google search, YouTube descriptions, Facebook and WhatsApp shares. Pakistani home cooking: desi measures (cups, tablespoons, "a handful"), grams, halal ingredients, family sized portions, Ramadan and Eid seasons, ingredients from the local market.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything is a demo: the cook, recipes, comments and ratings are made up and labelled as a demo where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- Recipe photos come from free stock libraries (Unsplash licence) and are credited.
- Interactive features run in the browser only: scaling servings, ticking steps, saving a recipe on the device, search and category filters. Newsletter sign up and comments are simulated.
- No health or medical claims about food.
- English recipe text, with Urdu dish names where they help (for example Chicken Karahi / کڑاہی).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real cook, reviews or photos. Photos are free stock images; recipes are written for the demo.

## Product Principles

1. Recipe first: the dish, time, servings and ingredients come before any story.
2. Cookable on a phone: big readable steps, tick-off progress, nothing that jumps around.
3. Honest demo: never let a visitor believe the cook or the ratings are real.
4. Fast and calm: light pages, no pop-ups, no clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large readable type for the kitchen, strong contrast, real alt text on food photos, works on slow connections.
